Education

Excel@NCL Supplementary School

The supplementary school of Nigerian Community Leeds started on Saturday 7th of July 2007. It gives peer support to children new to education in the UK, helps pupils prepare for SATs and GCSE in Maths, English and Science, and teaches self-identity through mother tongue, music, dance and story-telling.

  • Yoruba and Hausa classes
  • Dancing, drumming and tie and dye workshops
  • An African story books library of over 100 novels
  • Basic life skills, finance skills and citizenship

Sport

Leeds African Cup of Nations

LACoN is a one-day annual football tournament coordinated by Nigerian Community Leeds since 2007. Its main aim is to boost community cohesion, using football to bridge the gap between African communities in Leeds and the wider Leeds communities. Expect traditional African drumming, the best of African food, and supporters dressed in their national colours.

Welfare@NCL

Welfare encompasses everything NCL does to meet the personal and social requirements of its members. The Welfare Advisory Committee gives members first class advice on their enquiries and signposts them to relevant services. Around the festive season we also arrange an annual food donation scheme for members and families in need.

  • Services and employment support
  • Family, women's and men's support
  • Support for pensioners
