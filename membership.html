Join us

Become a Member

Nigerian Community Leeds is open to people who are Nigerian by birth, ancestry and descent, marriage, naturalisation or adoption, living in and around Leeds. We cater for people of all ages.

What members receive

  • Priority booking for our events and programmes
  • News of employment and educational opportunities
  • Information on workshops and support
  • Access to advice from the Welfare Advisory Committee

Questions before you join? Contact us or call 07786 967385.
