A Focal Point for Nigerians in the City of Leeds

There is a pressing need to have a focal point where Nigerians can converge in the City of Leeds and share their richly diverse cultural heritage with other Leeds residents. Unfortunately, NCL still has no operational base after 24 years.

Excel@NCL is a thriving supplementary Saturday school for Nigerian children, but it does not have its own space. Several small businesses have struggled with start-up costs and could benefit from shared office space and staff. Nigerians are always in search of venues to hire for various events.

Many older generation Nigerians face social isolation, as some of them have their offspring in different cities or even faraway countries. Nigeria House Leeds will provide a meeting point for social capital. It will also serve as an operational base for our mentoring programme for young persons of African descent, where they can safely explore positive alternatives and become young achievers.

What Nigeria House will offer

  • A home for the Excel@NCL Saturday school
  • Shared office space and staff for small businesses
  • Venues to hire for events
  • A Nollywood viewing area
  • Sports facilities such as a gym, table tennis and snooker
  • A library featuring Nigerian and other African writers
  • An African foods shop, bar and restaurant
  • A base for mentoring young people
