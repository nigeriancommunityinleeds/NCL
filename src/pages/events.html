Nigeria House Leeds Fundraising Gala 2026

A celebration of culture, community and change. Join us at a prestigious event dedicated to raising essential funds for the Nigeria House Leeds project. Why sponsor the gala

  • Foster cultural inclusion and celebrate African heritage
