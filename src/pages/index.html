What is our mission

We Support Nigerians In and Around Leeds

Nigerian Community Leeds is a registered charity for people who are Nigerian by birth, ancestry and descent, marriage, naturalisation or adoption. We cater for people of all ages and enable them to reach their full potential, contributing to health and socio-economic wellbeing in the city of Leeds.

How we are working

Education, Racial Harmony and Enterprise

NCL holds regular events to provide a platform for the furtherance of our aims.

We work towards enabling equality of access to support services for Nigerians, thereby ensuring the welfare and wellbeing of all Nigerians.

Led by our community

Meet Our Board of Trustees

Nigerian Community Leeds is a charitable organisation running under United Kingdom charity law. Our executives are elected into office every two years, led by a chairman and supported by officers for administration, finance, welfare and projects.
