Fundraising

Nigeria House Leeds Fundraising Gala 2026

A celebration of culture, community and change. Join us at a prestigious event dedicated to raising essential funds for the Nigeria House Leeds project. This is more than just a building: it is a legacy project, a beacon of hope, and a hub for cultural exchange, social action and economic empowerment.

Why sponsor the gala
  • Enhance corporate social responsibility with a community-driven initiative
  • Boost brand visibility across our community networks and public events
  • Foster cultural inclusion and celebrate African heritage
  • Contribute to a legacy project for future generations

Leeds African Cup of Nations

Our one-day annual football tournament. Come dressed in your national colours to cheer on your team, hear traditional African drumming and sample the best of African food. The date of the next tournament is to be announced.
